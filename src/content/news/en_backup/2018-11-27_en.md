A complete version of the Archaeological Database of Bohemia is available via the [link](http://www.archeologickamapa.cz/downloads/ADC2017.mdb) in the [Downloads](https://amcr-info.aiscr.cz/?page=documents) section.  
It includes all data submitted to ARÚP before the launch of AMCR.  
The database contains unrevised data, processed only with basic corrections.
