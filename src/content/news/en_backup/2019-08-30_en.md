All text documents stored in the archives of the Institute of Archaeology of the Czech Academy of Sciences in Prague are now available in the [AMCR Digital Archive](https://digiarchiv.aiscr.cz/). This marks the completion of the first stage of digitization and online access to the entire archive collection.
