# Planned downtime of the AMCR Digital Archive

In connection with the deployment of a new version of the Digital Archive, there will be approximately one hour of downtime on December 23 from 10 a.m. After that, an indexing of data content will take place, which will last approximately 48 hours. During this time, you may encounter partial data unavailability. We will inform you here when the process is complete.
