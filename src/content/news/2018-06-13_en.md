A new option is now available for submitting notifications regarding construction or other activities carried out in areas with archaeological finds, in accordance with Section 22 of Act No. 20/1987 Coll. on State Monument Care.  
The new online notification form is available at: <http://www.archeologickamapa.cz/oznameni>
